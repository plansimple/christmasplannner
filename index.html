Questions, answered

Will it work on my iPad?+

Yes. Import the PDF into GoodNotes or Notability and write over it. To type into the fields, use an app that supports PDF forms, such as the free Adobe Acrobat Reader app.

Can I print it at home?+

Yes. Print at 100% scale on US Letter or A4.

Is it dated?+

Only the December calendar page uses 2026 dates. Everything else is undated, so you can reuse it every year.

What do I receive?+

A digital download with two PDFs (US Letter and A4). No physical item is shipped.

Do the budget totals calculate automatically?+

Yes, in Adobe Acrobat and Reader. In other apps you can type the numbers in yourself.

Can I share or resell it?+

It’s for personal use. You may print as many copies as you need for your household, but please don’t share or resell the file.
